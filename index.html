Who I Am

Behind the kit, since day one

From tapping rhythms on the dining table as a kid to backing artists on stages across India and beyond.

Today Ashish splits his time between teaching the next generation of drummers — in his studio, at their homes and online — and performing with his own band, The Arctic Tern, and as a session player for other artists — mentored by Manoj Maveli, a Berklee College of Music (Boston, USA) alumnus who trains India's top drummers.

From a church conga to stages across India and abroad — Ashish has played for Yamaha, Kia and ASUS, performed Queen and Pink Floyd tribute shows with The Arctic Tern, and taught drummers aged 5 to 56.

The Journey

Four turning points,
one drummer.

The dining table

Random beats during family prayer. In 3rd grade, his first bongo. Then, every Sunday at church, a few minutes of mentoring from the conga player he sat behind — and when that player moved away, Ashish took over, and added the dholak.

A kit, and YouTube

The church received a drum kit, and Ashish played drums for the first time — influenced by Casey Cooper, Matt McGuire and Luke Holland.

Three months, every gig

A senior recommended him to artists, then left the city. Ashish covered all of his gigs — and came away with a network, a reputation and a whole new level of learning.

10+ bands

A cajon, then a first electronic kit he saved up for, then an acoustic set — and a rented place with his bandmates. The journey continues.
Along the way

B.Sc. (Hons) Neuroscience. M.A. Clinical Psychology. And gigs in bars, cafés and college fests.
