The Arctic Tern

Formed in December 2019, The Arctic Tern began when founding members Ashish and Dave crossed paths while playing with different artists and discovered a shared musical chemistry. A few months later, lead guitarist Marcus joined through a mutual friend, and the three have been at the core of the band ever since.

While the band began by exploring different styles, their true rock & roll journey took shape in 2022 through a series of gigs in Rishikesh, where they found an audience of travellers from across the world who shared their love for classic rock. It was there that The Arctic Tern found its sound, identity and niche.

The band performs music from legendary acts such as Led Zeppelin, Pink Floyd, The Doors, Queen and The Rolling Stones, alongside a growing catalogue of original music. Over the years, they have performed across India and internationally, including multiple Queen and Pink Floyd tribute shows and rock nights.
